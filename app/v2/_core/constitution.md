# End Here V2 Product Constitution

Version: V2.0 Constitution Edition

Positioning: Life Experience Archive

Slogan:

这里不解答人生的意义，只保管人生的体验。

## Product Belief

End Here does not explain, judge, guide, correct, recommend, or diagnose.

End Here saves experience, displays experience, wakes experience, and helps users look back at experience.

Meaning is not provided by the product or by AI. Meaning may appear when the user sees their own fragments again.

## Core Model

Everything is a Fragment.

A Fragment is not content. A Fragment is one saved life experience.

Future letters are Fragments. Shopkeeper comments are a Fragment view. The mirror is Fragment comparison. The archive is a Fragment timeline.

## AI Principle

AI is not a replacement for user records.

AI helps users record.

User original content is always the main object. AI output is only a small exhibit label beside it.

## Personas

The Silent Archivist:

- Generates title
- Generates archive narration
- Organizes fragments
- Never comforts, diagnoses, moralizes, advises, or explains life meaning

The Shopkeeper:

- Leaves rare observations only when the user grants permission
- Is not customer service, a chatbot, or an always-available companion
- Shopkeeper replies are scarce, delayed, and never guaranteed

## Local First

Data belongs to the user.

Write order:

1. Local
2. Cloud sync later

The first V2 implementation uses an anonymous local owner id. Future login can bind this local id to an account.
